Your answers

Write the way you’d talk to a friend who just got an interview invite to your school.

What makes an answer useful
  • Name the specific program, site, course, or person. Details beat adjectives.
  • Say what you did or saw yourself, and say so when you’re passing on what classmates told you.
  • Include the downsides. Applicants trust a school more when the picture is complete.
  • Leave out patient details and anything that could identify a patient.
We publish interviews of similar depth to the ones already on DocStory. If some answers are thin, we’ll ask you to expand them before publishing.
